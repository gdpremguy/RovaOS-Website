Rova Horizon
============

Created for RovaOS using original geometric curves and gradients.
AI-assisted design and implementation; no third-party photograph, stock image,
or existing wallpaper was used as input.

Editable source: rova-horizon.svg
Reproducible renderer: tools/build_horizon_wallpaper.py (requires Pillow)
Desktop export: rova-horizon.png, 3840 x 2160

This provenance note applies only to Rova Horizon, not to other artwork,
application icons, fonts, or the Arc theme used by the desktop. Their original
licences and attribution requirements still apply.
